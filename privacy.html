Privacy Policy

Dobby household assistant, operated by Nikhil Dixit (Sole Proprietor). Effective October 12, 2026.

Who this covers

Dobby is a private household assistant used only by Nikhil Dixit and spouse, members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

  • The mobile phone numbers of the two household members who opted in.
  • The content and timestamps of text messages exchanged with the assistant.
  • Calendar events, to-do list items, and details from the users' own email that the assistant needs to manage their calendar and lists.

How we use it

Only to provide the assistant to these two users: answering their requests, confirming changes, suggesting tasks or events for their approval, and sending occasional reminders. It is never used for marketing or advertising.

How it is stored and shared

Data is stored on a private device in the household. It is shared only with service providers needed to run the assistant (an SMS provider to deliver texts and an AI provider to process requests), and never for marketing.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Either user can ask for their data to be deleted at any time.
